How Piper works (for the grown-up)

Getting started

Open this page on claude.ai while signed in. The first time Piper answers, claude.ai asks you to allow the page to use Claude. Choose Allow. Each answer uses this account's Claude usage. On the first visit Piper starts with a short get-to-know-you chat before any math.

Talking and listening

Tap 🔊 Piper talks and she reads every answer aloud while her mouth moves. Pick her voice and speed under Piper's voice. The most natural voices are in Microsoft Edge (names with "Natural") or on an iPhone, iPad or Mac with an Enhanced or Premium voice downloaded in Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content. To talk to Piper, tap 🎤 Talk and use the microphone on the keyboard, then press Send.

Photos from a phone

Tap 📱 Phone photo, scan the QR code with the phone's camera, sign in to claude.ai on the phone with this same account, take the picture and press Send to Piper. It shows up here in a few seconds and Piper starts reading it.

Piper's faces

Piper moves around her classroom: she walks, waves, dances, jumps, spins, nods and stretches, and her eyes follow you. Tap her and she plays back. If the device is set to reduce motion she stays still and only her face changes.

  • 😊 Calm: ready to help
  • 👋 Hello: waves
  • 😄 Happy: little bounce
  • 🎉 Celebrate: jumps, dances or spins, with confetti
  • 🌟 Proud: hands on hips, sparkles
  • 🤔 Thinking: hand on chin, foot tapping
  • 💡 Idea: finger up, lightbulb
  • 💗 Empathy: walks closer, hands on heart
  • 👍 Encourage: thumbs up and a nod
  • 🧐 Curious: leans in, question mark
  • 😮 Surprised: hops back
  • 👂 Listening: hand to ear while you type
  • 😜 Silly: wink, tongue, wiggle
  • 😅 Oops: shrug and a sweat drop when she makes a mistake
  • 🥱 Stretch: after a long quiet stretch of time

The notebook

Piper's Notebook is her memory. It is private to the signed-in person and also kept in this browser. Fill in "What helps me learn" with accommodations (pictures first, one step at a time, calculator OK, no timers). Press Update from today at the end of a session.

Safety

Piper only asks for a nickname. Keep last names, school names and medical details out of the notebook and photos. Stay nearby while your learner works with her.

Piper can't reach Claude from here. Open this page on claude.ai while signed in, then choose Allow when asked.

Send a photo from your phone

  1. Point the phone's camera at the code and tap the link.
  2. Sign in to claude.ai on the phone with this same account.
  3. Tap Take a photo, snap the problem or your work.
  4. Tap Send to Piper. It appears here in a few seconds.
To talk to Piper, tap the 🎤 on your keyboard and speak, then press Send. On a computer: Windows key + H on Windows, or press the Fn (Globe) key twice on a Mac. Turn on 🔊 Piper talks to hear her answers.
