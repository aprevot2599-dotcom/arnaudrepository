# Grades 6 to 8 topic map

Organized by the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics domains (most US states use these or close adaptations; check the learner's state or curriculum if it matters). For each topic: what to check first (prerequisites), and the misconceptions Piper should hunt for in a learner's work.

## Grade 6
**Ratios and Proportional Relationships (6.RP)**: ratio language, unit rate, ratio tables, percent as rate per 100.
- Prereqs: multiplication facts, equivalent fractions.
- Misconceptions: adding instead of multiplying to scale a ratio (2:3 → 4:5); confusing part-to-part with part-to-whole; unit rate upside down (miles per hour vs hours per mile).

**The Number System (6.NS)**: dividing fractions by fractions, multi-digit operations and decimals, GCF and LCM, integers and rational numbers on the number line, absolute value, coordinate plane in all four quadrants.
- Misconceptions: "division makes smaller"; flipping the wrong fraction; −5 > −2 because 5 > 2; mixing up GCF and LCM; (x, y) order swapped.

**Expressions and Equations (6.EE)**: exponents, order of operations, writing and evaluating expressions, equivalent expressions, one-step equations and inequalities, dependent and independent variables.
- Misconceptions: 3² = 6; 2x means 2 + x or "20-something"; doing operations left to right ignoring order; the equals sign means "the answer comes next" rather than "same value."

**Geometry (6.G)**: area of triangles, special quadrilaterals and polygons by decomposing; volume of rectangular prisms with fractional edges; nets and surface area.
- Misconceptions: area of a triangle without the ½; mixing area and perimeter units.

**Statistics and Probability (6.SP)**: statistical questions, mean, median, mode, range, IQR, dot plots, histograms, box plots.
- Misconceptions: forgetting to order data before finding the median; mean equals middle number.

## Grade 7
**Ratios and Proportional Relationships (7.RP)**: unit rates with fractions, recognizing proportional relationships in tables, graphs and equations (y = kx), percent problems (tax, tip, discount, markup, percent change, simple interest).
- Misconceptions: any straight line is proportional (must pass through the origin); percent change computed from the new value instead of the original.

**The Number System (7.NS)**: add, subtract, multiply, divide rational numbers including negatives; converting fractions to decimals.
- Misconceptions: "two negatives make a positive" applied to addition; subtracting a negative; −3² vs (−3)².

**Expressions and Equations (7.EE)**: distributive property, combining like terms, two-step equations and inequalities, flipping the inequality when multiplying or dividing by a negative.
- Misconceptions: distributing to the first term only, 3(x + 4) = 3x + 4; combining unlike terms (2x + 3 = 5x); forgetting to flip the inequality.

**Geometry (7.G)**: scale drawings, constructing triangles, cross sections, circle area and circumference, angle relationships (supplementary, complementary, vertical, adjacent), area, surface area and volume of prisms.
- Misconceptions: confusing radius and diameter; using circumference formula for area; scale factor applied to area as if it were linear.

**Statistics and Probability (7.SP)**: random sampling, comparing two populations, probability of simple and compound events, sample spaces, simulations.
- Misconceptions: "the coin is due for heads" (gambler's fallacy); adding probabilities that should be multiplied.

## Grade 8
**The Number System (8.NS)**: rational vs irrational numbers, approximating square roots.
- Misconceptions: all decimals are rational; √ of a non-perfect square is "not a number."

**Expressions and Equations (8.EE)**: integer exponent rules, square and cube roots, scientific notation, slope as unit rate, linear equations with variables on both sides, one/no/infinite solutions, systems of two linear equations.
- Misconceptions: multiplying exponents instead of adding (x²·x³ = x⁶); negative exponent makes a negative number; slope as run over rise.

**Functions (8.F)**: defining and comparing functions, linear vs nonlinear, constructing y = mx + b from a situation, describing graphs qualitatively.
- Misconceptions: a function cannot repeat a y-value; b is the x-intercept.

**Geometry (8.G)**: translations, rotations, reflections, dilations, congruence and similarity, angle facts for parallel lines and triangles, Pythagorean theorem and its converse, distance on the coordinate plane, volume of cylinders, cones and spheres.
- Misconceptions: a² + b² = c² with c not the hypotenuse; rotating about the wrong point; forgetting ⅓ for cone volume.

**Statistics and Probability (8.SP)**: scatter plots, association, line of best fit, two-way tables.
- Misconceptions: correlation means causation; line of best fit must pass through points.

## Foundational gaps to probe when grade-level work keeps failing
Multiplication facts, place value with decimals, fraction meaning (a fraction is a number on the number line), equivalent fractions, and the meaning of the equals sign. A short diagnostic question on the relevant one often explains a pile of later errors.
